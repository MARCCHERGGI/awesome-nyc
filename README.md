## 🏠 Finding an Apartment

### Best Sites
- **[StreetEasy](https://streeteasy.com)** — Best filters, full NYC coverage, most listings
- **[Facebook Marketplace](https://facebook.com/marketplace)** — No-fee rooms, direct landlords, best deals
- **[SpareRoom](https://spareroom.com)** — Shared apartments, rooms, no broker fee
- **[Craigslist NYC](https://newyork.craigslist.org/search/apa)** — Still useful, vet carefully
- **[Nextdoor](https://nextdoor.com)** — Neighborhood-specific word of mouth

### Real Cost Breakdown
- First month + last month + security = **3 months rent upfront**
- Broker fee = **15% of annual rent** (often $2,000–4,000 extra)
- Average shared room (2025): $900–1,400/mo depending on borough

### Best Neighborhoods by Price
| Neighborhood | Avg Room | Transit |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Ridgewood, Queens | $900–1,100 | M/L train |
| Crown Heights, Brooklyn | $950–1,200 | 2/3/4/5 |
| Bushwick, Brooklyn | $1,000–1,300 | L train |
| Astoria, Queens | $1,100–1,400 | N/W |
| Upper Harlem | $1,000–1,300 | A/B/C/D |

## 💰 Budgeting

### Minimum Viable Budget (NYC)
```
Rent (shared room, outer borough):  $900
MetroCard unlimited:                 $132
Groceries (cooking):                 $150
Phone (Mint Mobile):                  $35
Laundry:                              $30
Misc / emergency buffer:             $100
─────────────────────────────────────────
Minimum monthly:                   $1,347
```

### Cheapest Grocery Stores
- **Aldi** — Cheapest overall
- **Western Beef / Compare Foods** — Best meat prices, outer boroughs
- **Trader Joe's** — Consistent pricing, good frozen meals
- **International markets** — Cheapest produce by far

### Phone Bills
Switch to **[Mint Mobile](https://mintmobile.com)** ($15–35/month on T-Mobile's network). If you're paying $80+/month, you're wasting $50+/month.

## 💼 Getting Your First Job

### Fastest Paths to Income
| Method | Time to First Paycheck | Earning Range |
|--------|----------------------|---------------|
| Restaurant (walk-in) | 3–7 days | $18–50/hr with tips |
| DoorDash / Uber Eats | 24–48 hours | $600–1,500/mo |
| Marketplace flipping | Same day | $200–800/weekend |
| TaskRabbit | 1 week | $35–75/hr |
| Temp agency | 1–2 weeks | $18–30/hr |

### The Walk-In Script That Works
Show up at restaurants **between 2–4pm** (after lunch, before dinner prep). Ask for the manager. Say:

> *"Hi, I'm looking for a [server/busser/host] position. Is the manager available for just 5 minutes?"*

Print 15 resumes. Hit 15 restaurants. Expect 3–5 callbacks.

### Certifications That Help
- **NYC Food Handler Certificate** — $15, online, takes 2 hours. Required for food service, gets you hired faster.
- **TIPS certification** — For bartending/bar work. Also cheap and online.

## ⚡ Side Hustles

### Week 1 — Start Immediately
- **DoorDash** — Sign up now, active in 24–48 hours. $20+/hr peak hours.
- **Facebook Marketplace flipping** — Source free items, relist for cash. Zero investment.
- **PC/phone repair** — If you have the skill, post on Nextdoor + local groups. $50–150/job.

### Month 1 — Build These
- **TaskRabbit** — $35–75/hr after approval. Best ROI per hour in the gig economy.
- **Freelance social media** — Small local businesses pay $300–500/month for Instagram management.
- **AI content services** — Use Claude + ElevenLabs to produce content for businesses. $500–1,500/mo.

### Month 2+ — Scale These
- **Digital products** — Write a guide, sell on Gumroad. Passive income, zero fulfillment.
- **Affiliate marketing** — Recommend tools you use. Best AI affiliate programs pay 22–50% recurring.
- **Content creation** — Build an audience documenting your NYC journey. Monetize with brand deals.

## 🚇 Transportation

- **MetroCard Unlimited** — $132/month. Best deal if you commute daily.
- **Pay-per-ride** — $2.90/ride. Better if you work remote/irregular.
- **Citi Bike** — $15/month for e-bike access. Game-changer for under 3-mile trips.
- **Uber/Lyft** — Expensive ($20–40 cross-town). Use sparingly.

### Key Transit Tips
- Download the **MTA app** — real-time train status
- **Google Maps** > Apple Maps for transit in NYC
- Outer borough to Manhattan: budget 30–60 minutes
